This week: CONSORT, Item 13 – Participant flow.
Which checklist item do you see ignored most often?

References:
• Schulz, K. F., Altman, D. G., & Moher, D. (2010). CONSORT 2010 statement: Updated guidelines for reporting parallel group randomised trials. BMJ, 340, c332. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.c332

Key points:
1. What the item asks: Report how many people were assessed, randomised, treated, lost and analysed, in each group: a flow diagram is strongly recommended.
2. Why it matters: Losses after randomisation can bias results. A trial that randomised 120 but analysed 80 has a story the reader must see.
3. How to comply: Build the flow diagram from your screening log before writing: numbers at every stage, with reasons for every exclusion.

Takeaway: Report the flow of all participants, stage by stage, with reasons for every exclusion.
