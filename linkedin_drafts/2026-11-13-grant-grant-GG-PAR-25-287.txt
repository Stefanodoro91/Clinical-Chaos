Key points:
1. What it funds: This R01 supports engineering development of novel brain stimulation devices or significant hardware/software enhancements to FDA-approved or cleared devices for mental health disorders.
2. Who it is for: Multidisciplinary teams combining engineering, device development, regulatory, computational sciences, neuroscience, and neuropsychiatry expertise. Eligible organizations include higher education, nonprofits, for-profits, governments, and foreign entities. Any qualified individual may serve as PD/PI.
