Knowledge intermediaries sought to validate Horizon innovations

Health-focused knowledge intermediaries: this call funds validation of Horizon Europe health innovations.

Consortia must scout Horizon results and run open calls for prototyping and market testing.
At least 60% of EU funding goes directly to third‑party validation projects.
Mentorship, training, and follow‑on funding access are included.
Are you planning to apply or know similar calls?

Programme: Horizon Europe
Eligible countries: EU Member States and associated countries (official call: “EU programme rules: legal entities established in EU Member States (including Italy) and associated countries are eligible.”)
Deadline: 16 Feb 2027

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Projects scout Horizon 2020 Pillar III and Horizon Europe Pillar II results, launch competitive validation calls, and direct ≥60% EU funds to third‑party prototyping, testing, piloting, and market replication.
2. Who it is for: Consortia of knowledge intermediaries (tech transfer offices, incubators, clusters) addressing one thematic area (Agriculture, Clean transition, Health, Bioeconomy, Raw materials) with a gender‑sensitive approach.

Takeaway: Knowledge intermediaries in Agriculture, Clean transition, Health, Bioeconomy, or Raw materials should apply to validate Horizon results.
