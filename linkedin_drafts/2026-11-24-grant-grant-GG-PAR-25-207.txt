Post-acute mental health care: funds for definitive clinical trials

Testing post-acute mental health interventions? This call supports definitive effectiveness trials for optimal care.

Collaborations between academic researchers and clinical or community practice partners or networks are encouraged.

Programme: NIH – US National Institutes of Health
Eligible countries: Any country (non-US organisations eligible) (official call: “Non-domestic (non-U.S.) Entities (Foreign Organizations) are eligible to apply.”)
Who can lead: Any individual with the skills, knowledge, and resources necessary to carry out the research as PD/PI is eligible.
Next deadline: 5 Feb 2027 (new applications); later deadlines: 5 Jun 2027, 5 Oct 2027
Official call: https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/356924
All open calls, grouped by programme: https://clinicalresearchchaos.com/calls#c-GG-PAR-25-207.

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Definitive clinical trials evaluating post-acute therapeutic and service delivery interventions for mental health conditions in youth, adults, and older adults.
2. Who it is for: Mental health researchers collaborating with clinical or community practice partners or networks.
