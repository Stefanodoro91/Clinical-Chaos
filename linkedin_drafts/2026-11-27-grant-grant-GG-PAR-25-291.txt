Planning invasive neural recording studies in humans? This call funds mental health circuit research. 🧠

This call supports exploratory R21 projects leveraging intra-operative, sub-acute, or chronic human invasive neural recording and stimulation. It targets human neural circuitry underlying complex moods, cognition, and behaviors relevant to psychiatric conditions. Key points:
1. What it funds: High-risk exploratory research utilizing invasive human neural recording and stimulation opportunities to clarify neural circuitry in mental health disorders.
2. Who it is for: Multidisciplinary teams that may include neurosurgery, electrophysiology, neuroscience, engineering, psychiatry, biostatistics, ethics, and psychology, with the expertise needed for rigorous invasive human studies.
