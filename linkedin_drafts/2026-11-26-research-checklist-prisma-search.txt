PRISMA item 7: a systematic review search must be reported so fully that anyone can repeat it.

One reporting-checklist item at a time, every Thursday: what it asks, why it matters, and how to comply in practice.
This week: PRISMA, Item 7 – Search strategy.
Which checklist item do you see ignored most often?

Key points:
1. What the item asks: Present the full search strategy for every database and register, including filters and limits, exactly as run.
2. Why it matters: The search defines the evidence base. If it cannot be repeated, the review cannot be checked or updated.
3. How to comply: Export each database's full query string into an appendix, with the date of the search and any language or date limits.

Takeaway: Report every search string, database, filter and date: reproducible or it is not systematic.
