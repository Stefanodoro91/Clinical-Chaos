This week: CONSORT, Item 14 – Outcomes.
Which checklist item do you see ignored most often?

Key points:
1. What the item asks: Report every prespecified primary and secondary outcome: measurement variable, analysis metric, method of aggregation and time point.
2. Why it matters: Vague outcomes invite switching after the data are in, one of the best-documented sources of reporting bias.
3. How to comply: Example: pain on a 0–10 numeric scale (variable), change from baseline (metric), mean (aggregation), at 12 weeks (time point).

Takeaway: An outcome is prespecified only if variable, metric, aggregation and time point are written first.
