Disability researchers: call for solutions for independent living

Researching independent living for persons with disabilities? This call supports innovative health and care solutions. ♿

This initiative supports research addressing physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory disabilities across the life-course. Projects must involve social sciences and humanities expertise and engage persons with disabilities through representative organisations. Are you planning a proposal in disability research, or do you know partners forming a consortium?

Programme: Horizon Europe
Eligible countries: EU Member States and associated countries (official call: “EU programme rules: legal entities established in EU Member States (including Italy) and associated countries are eligible.”)
Deadline: 17 Feb 2027
Official call: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-STAYHLTH-01
Looking for consortium partners? EU partner search: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/how-to-participate/partner-search (topic ID: HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-STAYHLTH-01)
All open calls, grouped by programme: https://clinicalresearchchaos.com/calls#c-EU-HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-STAYHLTH-01.

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Projects promoting independent living and community inclusion for persons with long-term physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory disabilities from a health perspective.
2. Who it is for: Disability research teams with social sciences and humanities experts collaborating with persons with disabilities through their representative organisations.
