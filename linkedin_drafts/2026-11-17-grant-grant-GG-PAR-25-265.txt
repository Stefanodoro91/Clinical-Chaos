Focusing on screenable disorders? Support for newborn research

Researching screenable disorders in newborns? This call funds novel screening tests and therapeutic interventions.

This program supports the development of innovative screening technologies and treatments for conditions already on newborn screening panels, as well as potentially fatal or disabling genetic conditions that could benefit from early detection. Are you working on newborn screening or related therapies?

Programme: Hunter Kelly Newborn Screening Research Program
Eligible countries: Any country (non-US organisations eligible) (official call: “Non-domestic (non-U.S.) Entities (Foreign Organizations) are eligible to apply.”)
Who can lead: Any individual with the skills, knowledge, and resources necessary to carry out the proposed research may be the PD/PI.
Next deadline: 5 Feb 2027 (new applications); later deadlines: 5 Jun 2027, 5 Oct 2027
Official call: https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357485
All open calls, grouped by programme: https://clinicalresearchchaos.com/calls#c-GG-PAR-25-265.

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Grants fund research on novel screening approaches and therapeutic interventions for disorders currently on or eligible for newborn screening panels.
2. Who it is for: Investigators with expertise in basic, behavioral, translational, or clinical research are invited to apply; see Section III of the NOFO for detailed eligibility criteria.
