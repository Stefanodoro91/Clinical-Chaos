Digital mental health researchers: funds for platform-based trials

Testing digital mental health interventions on established platforms? Learn what this R01 call supports 🧠

This initiative supports research projects to refine and evaluate existing evidence-based digital mental health interventions using rapid digital test beds.

Proposed studies must focus particularly on populations experiencing health disparities or vulnerable groups.

Are you planning to apply for this call, or do you know of similar digital health funding opportunities?

Programme: NIH R01 Research Project Grant
Eligible countries: Any country (non-US organisations eligible) (official call: “Non-domestic (non-U.S.) Entities (Foreign Organizations) are eligible to apply.”)
Who can lead: Any individual with the skills, knowledge, and resources necessary to carry out the proposed research may work with their organization to apply.
Next deadline: 5 Feb 2027 (new applications); later deadlines: 5 Jun 2027, 5 Oct 2027
Official call: https://www.grants.gov/search-results-detail/357034
All open calls, grouped by programme: https://clinicalresearchchaos.com/calls#c-GG-PAR-25-136.

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Supports development of digital health test beds to rapidly refine existing evidence-based interventions and conduct statistically powered clinical trials.
2. Who it is for: Requires partnerships between academic researchers and software developers leveraging well-established platforms with evidence-based interventions and ≥1,000 active users.
