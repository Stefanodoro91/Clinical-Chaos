Targeting child and youth NCDs? Support for implementation research

Studying child and youth non-communicable diseases? This call funds NCD implementation research.

This call supports implementation research exploring evidence-based interventions for chronic NCD prevention, diagnosis, screening, and management in children and young adults (1 to 24 years). Projects must focus on populations in LMICs or underserved communities in HICs, featuring equitable team leadership and early career co-investigators. Are you planning an implementation study on child and youth NCDs, or do you know a team that should apply?

Programme: Horizon Europe – Cluster 1: Health
Eligible countries: EU Member States and associated countries (official call: “EU programme rules: legal entities established in EU Member States (including Italy) and associated countries are eligible.”)
Who can lead: Expert multidisciplinary research team including at least one early career member as a co-investigator.
Deadline: 17 Feb 2027
Official call: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-DISEASE-10
Looking for consortium partners? EU partner search: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/how-to-participate/partner-search (topic ID: HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-DISEASE-10)
All open calls, grouped by programme: https://clinicalresearchchaos.com/calls#c-EU-HORIZON-HLTH-2027-01-DISEASE-10.

Details are compiled automatically from the official call: always check eligibility, amounts and dates before applying.

Key points:
1. What it funds: Implementation research on prevention, diagnosis, screening, and management of chronic NCDs in people aged 1 to 24 in LMICs or underserved populations in HICs.
2. Who it is for: Multidisciplinary teams with equitable HIC-LMIC or Indigenous partnerships, SSH experts, and an early career researcher as co-investigator, engaging stakeholders.
