Key points:
1. What the item asks: Define every pre-specified primary and secondary outcome: the measure, how it was assessed, and exactly when.
2. Why it matters: Vague outcomes invite switching after the data are in, one of the best-documented sources of reporting bias.
3. How to comply: Write each outcome as: instrument + metric + aggregation + time point. Example: pain on a 0–10 scale, mean change at 12 weeks.

Takeaway: An outcome is pre-specified only if measure, metric and time point are written down first.
