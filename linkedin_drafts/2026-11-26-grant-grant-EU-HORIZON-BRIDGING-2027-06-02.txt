Knowledge intermediaries: validate and valorise Horizon research

Active in knowledge valorisation? Support validation and commercialisation pathways for research results. 💡

This action addresses the structural gap between research outcomes and market deployment across five thematic areas (Agriculture and Food systems, Clean transition, Health, Bioeconomy, Raw materials & advanced materials). Consortia will provide expert mentorship, launch competitive calls for prototyping, testing, demonstrating, piloting, product validation and market replication, and guide selected candidates to real-life validation sites such as living labs, demonstration sites, and industrial partners.
