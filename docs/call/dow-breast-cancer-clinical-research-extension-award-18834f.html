The fiscal year 2026 Breast Cancer Research Program Clinical Research Extension Award supports research that extends data collection, follow-up, and analysis of breast cancer clinical studies to increase their clinically relevant impact. It funds deeper molecular analysis, correlative studies, biomarker validation, or patient follow-up. Independent investigators affiliated with any eligible organization worldwide can apply, and the research team must include consumer advocates and meet specific programmatic challenges.

From the official call: “Extramural and intramural U.S. Department of War (DOW) organizations are eligible to apply, including foreign and domestic organizations, for-profit and nonprofit organizations, and public or private entities.”
