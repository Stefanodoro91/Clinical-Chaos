NIH – US National Institutes of Health 1

UG3/UH3 Exploratory/Developmental Phased Award

Seamless Early-Stage Clinical Drug Development (Phase 1 to 2a) for Novel therapeutic Agents for AD/ADRD

This funding opportunity supports the early-stage clinical drug development of novel, non-amyloid and non-tau therapeutic candidates for Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. It funds phased projects that bundle phase 1 clinical trials (UG3) with phase 1b/2a trials (UH3) to evaluate safety, dosing, pharmacokinetics, and target engagement. Applications are open to higher education institutions, nonprofits, for-profit businesses, and foreign non-U.S. organizations.

Eligible countries: Any country (non-US organisations eligible)

From the official call

“Non-domestic (non-U.S.) Entities (Foreign Organizations) are eligible to apply.”

Michael J. Fox Foundation 1

Bridge Funding for Disrupted Neurodegenerative Research (BFDN) grant program

Bridge Funding for Disrupted Neurodegenerative Research Grant Program (BFDN) Powering the Next Generation of Research

This program supports early-career investigators whose research projects on Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and related neurodegenerative disorders were disrupted by operational or priority changes in U.S. federal funding. Grants provide up to $250,000 for 3 to 12 months to cover salary and direct research costs, requiring up to 25% institutional cost-sharing. Eligible principal investigators must meet NIH Early Stage Investigator definitions (or be within seven years of designation) and have experienced specific disruptions to federal grants.

US Department of Defense – CDMRP 6

Parkinson’s Research Program

DoW Parkinson's, Early Investigator Research Award

The FY26 Parkinson’s Research Program Early Investigator Research Award supports Parkinson’s disease research by investigators in the early stages of their careers. Proposed research must address at least one of four program focus areas; clinical trials are not allowed. Two funding levels are offered: Funding Level 1 for fellows (requiring guidance from an experienced mentor) and Funding Level 2 for early-career independent investigators (requiring preliminary data). Applicant eligibility is unrestricted.

Parkinson’s Research Program (PRP)

DoW Parkinson's, Investigator-Initiated Research Award

The FY26 Parkinson’s Research Program Investigator-Initiated Research Award supports rigorous, multidisciplinary research projects with the potential to make significant contributions to Parkinson’s disease research. Projects must address at least one FY26 PRP focus area, with clinical trials allowed and preliminary data required. Eligibility is unrestricted by entity type, and applications may include a Partnering Principal Investigator option with one initiating PI and up to two partnering PIs.

DoW Spinal Cord Injury, Investigator-Initiated Research Award

The FY26 Spinal Cord Injury Research Program Investigator-Initiated Research Award supports studies that make significant contributions to spinal cord injury research, patient care, or quality of life.
